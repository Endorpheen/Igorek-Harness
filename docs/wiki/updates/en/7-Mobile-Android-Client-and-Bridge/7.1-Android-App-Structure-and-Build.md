!!! info "Image thumbnails and background reconnects · Android 0.1.112 (113)"
    Selected images appear inside the composer with individual remove buttons. Uploaded-image metadata is retained in user messages, live/queued events and restored history. The app uses the existing authenticated, certificate-pinned upload download route, private cache files and bounded off-thread thumbnail decoding. Non-image files retain their names.

    Only `NET.READ_TIMEOUT` from the event stream (`mobile-events`, `sse_read`, read operation) may remain quiet while the activity is in the background or during its first 15 seconds after returning. The incident remains in diagnostic exports. If the stream has not recovered after that foreground grace period, its warning becomes visible. Authentication, TLS, model, send/write, ordinary foreground and other connection failures retain their notifications.

    Messages with thumbnails omit the redundant legacy attachment line, including restored history. Filenames remain below the previews.
