!!! info "OpenCode callers · 0.2.12"
    Distinguish the general sandbox defaults from the integrity selected by a caller. Both manual OpenCode and delegated assignments use **medium integrity** with restricted tokens and temporary original-project/service grants, preserving direct editing. The assignment's independent service does not weaken unrelated private-file boundaries. Internal regular file symlinks may resolve within the granted project; external and directory reparse points remain rejected. The x64 native pipe adapter, private desktop, Job Object and exact grant restoration remain in effect. See the [OpenCode Windows runner](https://github.com/Endorpheen/Igorek-Harness/blob/d0e210bbdb9db152c312dc2d089f0cf4f0b520d9/desktop/opencode-windows.py).
